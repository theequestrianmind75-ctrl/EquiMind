<product_requirements>
EquiMind is a mental performance mobile app for equestrians. Recent development has focused on enhancing the in-app experience and fixing critical usability issues.

A key feature, the During Ride page, was created to provide voice-activated coaching. It implements a system that limits riders to two tools per session for safety, with an increased limit of four for Breathing/HRV exercises. The page provides smart suggestions based on the user's pre-ride emotional state and includes an Emergency Tool that is always accessible, even without an active ride session.

Extensive UI/UX improvements were made across the Rider Tools category pages. This included renaming categories, adding custom educational summaries (e.g., for Emotional Regulation and Distress Tolerance), and implementing collapsible TOOLS and CRISIS TOOLS sections to prevent information overload. A consistent brand identity was established by making all interactive buttons a uniform hunter green.

A critical Forgot Password feature was also added to the authentication flow to address users being locked out of their accounts. The current priority is ensuring the text-to-speech voice selection works reliably on all devices.
</product_requirements>
