<product_requirements>
EquiMind is a mental performance mobile app for equestrians. It offers a library of mental strategies (Rider Tools) and personalized recommendations based on user-inputted emotional states before and after a ride.

The recent development cycle focused on several key areas:
1.  **Deployment Stability:** The primary goal was to fix recurring deployment failures caused by authentication and data synchronization issues, making the app stable and editable.
2.  **Content Expansion & Management:** The user added 15 new mindfulness tools, bringing the total to 205. A major requirement was ensuring that edits made to these tools in the admin editor would persist through deployments, which was achieved by creating a new data sync workflow. Numbered lists were also programmatically removed from tool instructions to improve formatting.
3.  **New Feature - Trend Analysis:** A Trends page was created to replace the old Ride tab. This page provides riders with long-term insights by tracking anxiety, confidence, and energy levels over the last 5 sessions, detecting patterns, and highlighting their best session.
4.  **UI/UX Refinements:** Numerous user-requested UI tweaks were implemented, including changing the TTS voice to a more natural female one, simplifying the strategy detail page, changing the auth screen logo color to black, and cleaning up icons and layouts on the new Trends page.
</product_requirements>
