## Trigger Conditions & Suggested Tools

### 1️⃣ HIGH ANXIETY (Post-ride anxiety ≥ 7)
**Goal**: Activate parasympathetic nervous system, reduce cortisol

**Recommendations**:
- 🧘 Try 'Box Breath' or '4:8 Tension Melt Exhale' from BODY RESET before your next ride to regulate your nervous system.
- 💡 Consider practicing 'The Safe Seat Reset' during rides when anxiety spikes - it activates your parasympathetic nervous system.

**Suggested Tools** (3 max):
1. `Double-Length Exhale (4:8)` - Breathing/HRV (3 min)
2. `Vagal Hum Exhale` - BODY RESET (3 min)
3. `Pre-Ride Anxiety Release` - Tapping/EFT (5 min)

---

### 2️⃣ ANXIETY INCREASED (Anxiety increased by 2+ points during ride)
**Goal**: Cognitive grounding, interrupt rumination, present-moment awareness

**Recommendations**:
- 🎯 Your anxiety increased during the ride. Try 'Cognitive Grounding Through Sensory Anchors' (identify 3 things you see, 2 things you feel) to stay present.
- 💭 Practice 'Thought Labeling + Releasing' - name the anxious thought and separate from it: 'That's an anxious thought, I don't have to act on it.'

**Suggested Tools** (3 max):
1. `Five-Sense Horseback Scan` - Mindfulness & Flow (5 min)
2. `Mindful Listening to Arena Sounds` - Mindfulness & Flow (5 min)
3. `Post-Ride Processing` - Tapping/EFT (6 min)

---

### 3️⃣ LOW CONFIDENCE (Post-ride confidence ≤ 4)
**Goal**: Build self-efficacy, create mastery experiences, positive reinforcement

**Recommendations**:
- 💪 Build confidence with 'Micro-Goal Cueing' - set small achievable goals like '5 steady strides' or 'one smooth corner.'
- 🌟 Try EFT Tapping sequences specifically for confidence building - research shows tapping reduces cortisol by 24%.

**Suggested Tools** (3 max):
1. `Quick Confidence Boost` - Tapping/EFT (3 min)

3. `HRV Coherence Breath (Inhale 4, Exhale 6)` - Breathing/HRV (3 min)
