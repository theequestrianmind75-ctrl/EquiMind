## 📱 First Time Setup

### Step 1: Download Expo Go
- **iPhone:** Download from [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/expo-go/id982107779)
- **Android:** Download from [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=host.exp.exponent)

### Step 2: Open the App

**Option A - Direct Link (Recommended):**
1. Open the **Expo Go** app
2. Tap the **search bar** at the top
3. Copy and paste this link:
   ```
   exp://mental-equestrian-1.preview.emergentagent.com
   ```
4. Tap **Go** or press Enter
5. The app will load! ✨

**Option B - QR Code:**
1. Open the **Expo Go** app
2. Tap **"Scan QR code"**
3. Scan the QR code (attached in the email/message)

## 🔄 Accessing the App Again

Once you've opened the app once, it's easy to get back in:

1. Open **Expo Go**
2. Look in the **"Recently opened"** or **"Projects"** section
3. Tap **EquiMind** to launch it instantly!

**Can't find it?** Just use the direct link or QR code again.

## ❓ Troubleshooting

**App won't load:**
- Make sure you have internet connection (WiFi or mobile data)
- Try closing Expo Go completely and reopening it
- Rescan the QR code or re-enter the link

**App disappeared from "Recently opened":**
- This is normal! Just use the link or QR code again
- Once you open it, it will reappear in your recent projects

**App is slow or buggy:**
- This is a development version, so some bugs are expected
- Please report any issues you find - that's what beta testing is for! 🙏
